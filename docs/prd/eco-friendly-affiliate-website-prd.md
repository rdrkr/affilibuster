# **Eco-Friendly Affiliate Website Product Requirements**

## **1. Core Page Types**

- Homepage
  - Highlights mission/brand promise of eco-friendliness.
  - Showcases featured products, special bundles, blog previews, and top categories.
- Category Pages
  - Organized by product type (e.g., Home, Personal Care, Fashion, Pets).
  - Filters for certifications, material, price range, brand, sustainability features.
- Product Review/Details Pages
  - Detailed product description, eco-certifications, price, ratings.
  - Affiliate links/buttons with clear CTAs (e.g., "Buy on [Merchant]").
  - Related products or bundle suggestions.
- Blog / Content Pages
  - Educational and SEO-focused content to attract organic traffic.
  - Articles, guides, tips to build expertise and authority.
- About / Mission Page
  - Brand story focused on sustainability values and affiliate transparency.
- Affiliate Disclosure & Privacy Pages
  - Legal compliance for affiliate disclosure and user privacy policies.
- Contact & Support Page
  - Contact form, FAQs, and user support resources.

## **2. Key UI Components/Blocks**

- Call to Actions buttons for Affiliate Link Tracker
  - Clicking affiliate links triggers tracking codes for correct attribution.
- Search and Smart Filters
  - Robust search box with auto-suggestions.
  - Multi-faceted filters by product attributes and sustainability certifications.
- Bundle/Kit Recommendations (future)
  - Curated product bundles shown with cross-links to merchants.
- Review & Rating System
  - User-generated and editorial reviews with ratings.
- Personalization Engine (Future)
  - Product recommendations based on user behavior and preferences.
- Multi-language Support
  - English, Italian, Hebrew with localized content and UX.
- Popups/Email subscribe blocks
  - Email campaigns, retargeting pixel integration.
    Login Area (can be build after)

## **3. User Stories**

- As a visitor, I want to browse eco-friendly products by category, so I can find relevant sustainable options easily.
- As a user, I want to read detailed reviews and see certification info, so I can confidently choose eco-friendly brands.
- As a user, I want to compare related products in a bundle, so I can get complete eco-friendly kits.
- As an admin, I want to add/edit affiliate programs and track link performance, so I can optimize affiliate partnerships.
- As a marketer, I want to publish SEO blog content easily, so I can attract organic traffic from my target languages.
- As a returning visitor, I want personalized product recommendations, so the site feels relevant to my interests.
- As a user, I want a fast, responsive site that loads well on mobile and desktop.

## **4. Internal Mechanisms & Tracking**

- Affiliate Link Management
  - Central dashboard to manage and update affiliate URLs.
- Click & Conversion Tracking
  - Integration with Google Analytics, search console. Possibility to have end to end tracking via ClickID postback technology
- Content Management System (CMS)
  - Flexible CMS for product pages, blogs, and SEO metadata.
- SEO Optimization
  - Schema markup for products and reviews.
  - Fast page loads (image optimization, caching, lazy loading).
- Multi-Language SEO
  - hreflang tags, localized keywords, and site structure.

## **5. Scaling & Future Enhancements(all future)**

- API-first Architecture
  - Ready for integration with third-party tools or merchants.
- Headless CMS Option
  - Separate backend and frontend for agile updates and multi-platform.
- Affiliate Program Automation
  - Auto-discovery of new affiliate deals and commission updates.
- Bundle Purchase System (Future)
  - Integrate APIs from merchants for combo offers and streamlined checkout.

## 6. Technical Requirements

- Use latest and greatest and most popular tech stack
- Implementation should be generic and modular in a way that it will be easy to build more sites with reusable components.
- Must follow Clean Architecture.
- Must follow SOLID principles.
